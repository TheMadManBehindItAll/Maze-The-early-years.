Maze: The early years.


🔍

This website is dedicated to a fanmade prequel (kind of) to Christopher Manson's 1985 book, Maze: solve the World's Most Challenging Puzzle made by me, for the sake of my anonymity I wont announce who I am, at least not at the moment. This website is also supposed to be stylized after the website by Al Sweigart, though the artstyle is much different, being inspired by the hit 2023 indie game Pizza Tower, I apologize if it looks a goofy sometimes; I'm still learning as an artist.


Just like that website, here, the rooms are implemented as clickable HTML image maps, but you are also free to move to any room by clicking the numbers at the bottom of each page, this is a book after all, FUN FACT: I actually learned HTML purely for this website, isn't it incredible how random passion projects can teach you meaningful skills?, oh, and by the way, you are free to use all the assets within this book, you don't even need to give credit, but don't pretend you made it, HOWEVER, under NO circumstances do you have permission to edit the images with AI or use them to train AI.


This book takes place long, LONG before the events of the original 1985 book, the Guide is younger, you should expect to see him act as such, the house is smaller now, you only have 20 rooms rather than 45, and your only task is to make it to room 20 from room 1, the riddles within are considerably easier, but they still require some brainpower, meaning that this book sort of functions as a semi tutorial of the original.

Without further ado...you may proceed.
