...Freedom.


The light was incredibly strong, but after we got adjusted to it, we saw the way out, my group was incredibly ecstatic to learn they were one of the very few to solve the Maze.


They gave me their thanks, then they left.


In all honesty, even though I find the people I tend to herd rather unlikeable, I still feel kind of pleasant when one of my groups manages to make it out of the Maze, though as time goes on, the Maze will get harder, so I won't see these victories as often.


I loved how the breeze felt.


The best pleasure of all though...


...was knowing that they didn't have to suffer the fate that many, many other groups here had to suffer.
