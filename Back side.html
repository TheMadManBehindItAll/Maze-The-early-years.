The maze...you may know it as the ultimate challenge that overconfident solvers will attempt to complete once and for all...only to be trapped within its monstrous walls for all eternity...you may also remember its strongly critical, snarky, but ultimately wise "Guide" if you can even call him that...however...everything big was once small, and all stories have a beginning.


Meet your old Guide, younger, immature, and quick to anger, he has much, much to learn, and he is quite a bit shorter...he certainly isnt the most pleasent individual to be around, however he can sometimes pull out a bit of his future self, the Guide is not the only thing here that is younger however...the house itself is far smaller, rather then 45 roms you will only have 20, not only that, its riddles that were once titanic in their difficulty are now considerably easier...though that does not mean they will be a complete pushover, they still will require a good deal of brain power, and never EVER, start wandering around aimlessly, it is highly likely you could fall into a place of dread...


Enter room 1, you see 3 doors, each with a certain amount of strikes on them, in the side of your view you will see an olympic symbol, a heart, and the number 4 with the japan flag next to it, is there a connection between it and the strikes on the doors? you pick room 8 and see 3 doors of varying sizes with a painting in front of you, what sort of correlation could they have? it is up to you to figure out, do not expect your "Guide" to help you too much, he will not get the ability to talk to you directly at here until he gets better...but just one last thing...


DO. NOT. ENTER. THE ROOM WITH IT.
